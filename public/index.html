HOW TO PLAY

Crack the competition. Most eliminations when the timer hits zero wins. Free For All is every egg for itself. Team Deathmatch is MAD vs EGGS.

Random abilities. Every match, every player gets a hidden ability from a shared pool. It is not tied to your character. You find out at the ABILITY REVEAL. Fill the gold ring by waiting, dealing damage and grabbing Ability Egg-nergy, then tap the ability button.

Move with the left stick. Aim with the right stick (it fires when pushed far, if auto-fire is on). FIRE, JUMP (hop over low crates), RELOAD, and USE to grab weapons from the floor.

Pickups: health, shield, speed, damage boost, ability charge, and the Mystery Egg (anything can happen, some of it is bad).

On desktop: WASD move, mouse aim and shoot, Space jump, Q ability, R reload, E use, Tab scoreboard.

Tip: landscape feels best on a phone.
